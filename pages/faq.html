Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about our platform

General Questions

What is Bankers Association? ▼
Bankers Association is a comprehensive platform for banking professionals that provides exam preparation resources, transfer support, community forums, and current affairs updates.
Who can join this platform? ▼
Currently, our platform is designed for SBI employees. We verify users through their official email addresses and employee IDs to ensure a trusted community.
Is the platform free to use? ▼
Yes! Core features including question banks, transfer marketplace, and community forums are completely free. We believe in supporting the banking community.

Exam Preparation

How many questions are available? ▼
We currently have 500+ practice questions covering banking awareness, reasoning, quantitative aptitude, and general knowledge. We regularly add new questions.
Are the questions verified? ▼
Yes, all questions are reviewed and verified by experienced banking professionals and subject matter experts before being added to our database.
Can I track my progress? ▼
Absolutely! Your dashboard shows detailed analytics including questions practiced, accuracy rate, weak areas, and performance trends over time.

Transfer Support

How does the transfer marketplace work? ▼
You can post your transfer requirements (location you want to move from/to) and browse listings from others. When you find a match, you can contact them directly through the platform.
Is my contact information visible to everyone? ▼
No, your contact information is only shared when you choose to connect with someone. You have full control over your privacy settings.
Do you help with the transfer process? ▼
We provide a platform to connect with potential transfer partners and access transfer policies. The actual transfer process is handled through official bank channels.

Community & Forums

Can I post anonymously in forums? ▼
Currently, all forum posts show your name to maintain accountability and trust. However, you can control what personal information is visible in your profile.
How are forums moderated? ▼
We have community guidelines and a team of moderators who review reported content. Inappropriate posts, spam, or harassment will result in content removal and potential account suspension.

Technical Questions

Is there a mobile app? ▼
We're currently working on mobile apps for iOS and Android. In the meantime, our website is fully mobile-responsive and works great on all devices.
I forgot my password. What should I do? ▼
Click on "Forgot Password" on the login page. We'll send you a password reset link to your registered email address.
How do I delete my account? ▼
You can delete your account from your profile settings. Please note that this action is permanent and cannot be undone.
